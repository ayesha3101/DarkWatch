        How to read this

        Some fishing boats switch off their tracking (AIS). A patrol with limited fuel has to decide where to look.

        • Green glow is the patrol's belief: where a hidden boat might be. It spreads as time passes and shrinks when a search finds nothing.
        • White dot is a boat that still broadcasts AIS, so the patrol knows where it is.
        • Dashed ring is the last place a boat was seen before it went dark.
        • Green arrow is the patrol. The ring around it is its fuel, and the dashed square is what its sensor can see.
        • Tick marks a boat the patrol found.
        • Dark green tiles show where fishing is common (real data). Grey blocks are land.
